Most of my week is spent in front of customers, not in front of a slide deck.
I work with healthcare organizations, and they always ask me the same question.
Can this actually run in production, or is it only a demo?

That is a fair question. So I build the thing.
I would rather show a working system than describe one.

What surprises people is how much of the work is not the model at all.
It is the integration. Identity, data boundaries, who is allowed to see what.
The interesting part is never the part that looks interesting.

Do I get it right the first time? Almost never.
I ship something small, I watch where it breaks, and I fix it.
Then I do that again.

I like this work because the feedback is honest.
The code runs, or it does not. The customer trusts it, or they do not.
There is not much room to argue with that.
